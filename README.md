## 📌 Executive Summary & The Problem

Digital marketers, creators, entrepreneurs, and startup teams are trapped in **The Fragmented Content Stack**:
- **Tool Fatigue:** Jumping across 6+ disjointed applications (ChatGPT for drafting, Grammarly for editing, Hemingway for readability, Buffer for scheduling, Ahrefs for SEO, and Notion for archiving).
- **Tone Drift & Generic AI Slop:** Standard LLM wrappers produce robotic, cliché-ridden text with zero brand persona memory, eroding audience trust.
- **The Repurposing Bottleneck:** Reformatting a single blog post into LinkedIn thought leadership, X threads, Instagram carousels, and email newsletters takes hours of manual rewriting.
- **Hallucinations & Ungrounded Claims:** Unverified statistical assertions and robotic tropes slip into customer-facing copy unnoticed.

### 💡 The Solution: Writara
**Writara** replaces the fragmented stack with an all-in-one **Autonomous Content Operating System**. It connects deep prompt engineering, persistent Brand Voice DNA memory, a 1-click 6-channel repurposer, an anti-hallucination factual radar, Google SERP simulation, and an executive ROI velocity dashboard into a unified, zero-latency desktop studio.

### 1. 🎙️ Brand Voice Memory & Tone DNA Reverse-Engineering
*No more copy-pasting custom system prompts.*
- **Reverse-Engineer Any Style:** Paste raw writing samples, brand manifestos, or top-performing essays. Writara automatically extracts formality ratings (0–100), emotion scores, key syntax rules, and banned vocabulary (*e.g., "synergy", "game-changer", "delve"*).
- **Persistent Persona Memory:** Store unlimited distinct brand profiles (e.g., *SaaS Founder, Technical Educator, Witty Marketer*). Selected profiles automatically inject active constraints into downstream generation and editing workflows.

### 2. 🔁 1-Click Multi-Platform Repurposing Matrix
*Turn 1 master asset into an omnichannel digital presence instantly.*
A single source draft transforms simultaneously into **6 channel-native assets**:
1. **LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post:** Hook-first formatting, breathable whitespace, thought-provoking questions, and engagement CTA.
2. **X / Twitter Viral Thread:** High-CTR opener hook, numbered progressive insights, and concluding bookmark prompt.
3. **Instagram Carousel & Caption:** Slide-by-slide visual concepts, text bites, and algorithmic hashtag clouds.
4. **Email Newsletter:** Dual high-open subject lines, preview snippet, personal narrative arc, and clear conversion button.
5. **Short-Form Video Script (Reels/TikTok/Shorts):** Timestamped visual direction, B-roll cues, and spoken audio scripts.
6. **Executive Brief:** Key strategic takeaways, impact analysis, and actionable next steps for stakeholders.

### 3. 🛡️ Anti-Hallucination Fact Radar & Quality Evaluator
- **Anti-Hallucination Radar:** Algorithmic regex and semantic heuristics isolate ungrounded statistical claims, percentages, and historical dates, highlighting them for creator verification before publication.
- **Flesch-Kincaid Readability Analysis:** Real-time Reading Ease (0–100), Grade Level equivalency, and sentence cadence metrics.
- **Triple-Vector Alignment:** Simultaneously computes prompt adherence score, tone fidelity score, and clarity percentage.

### 4. 📈 Interactive Google SERP Simulator & Schema.org JSON-LD
- **Live Search Engine Simulation:** Real-time desktop and mobile Google SERP snippet previews with dynamic character length gauges (Title < 60 chars, Meta Description < 155 chars).
- **Automated Schema Markup:** Instantly generates valid, copy-ready `Schema.org Article` JSON-LD structured data for rich snippets.
- **Keyword Prominence Matrix:** Evaluates target keyword presence across H1, early paragraph content, headings, and page URL slug.

### 5. 🧪 Headline A/B Testing Matrix & AI Cliché Scanner
- **5 Viral Headline Formulas:** Algorithmic generation across proven psychological angles (*How-To / Blueprint, Contrarian Myth-Buster, Data-Driven Numbers, Pain-Point / FOMO, Transformational Outcome*) complete with predicted CTR scores.
- **AI Cliché Scanner:** Flags robotic AI tropes (*"In today's fast-paced world"*, *"delve into"*, *"testament to"*, *"unlock the power"*, *"pivotal role"*) and supplies organic human alternatives.

### 6. 📅 Multi-Channel Content Calendar & Scheduler
- Visual editorial timeline organizing queued content across LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Email, Blog, and YouTube.
- Status management (*Draft, Scheduled, Published*) with interactive stage transitions and direct handoffs back into the editor.

### 7. 💰 Content Velocity & Copywriter Cost ROI Dashboard
- Quantifies commercial ROI in real time: tracks total words generated, hours of manual labor saved, and estimated cost savings based on standard agency copywriting rates ($0.15/word).

### 8. 🌓 Dual Theme Engine (Dark Studio & Academic Light Mode)
- One-click navbar toggle between a sleek, distraction-free **Dark Studio Mode** and a clean, high-contrast **Light Mode** optimized for white-background academic and corporate reporting.

### 9. 📄 Multi-Format Exporter
- Export any finalized draft to **Word DOCX**, **Print-to-PDF**, **Clean HTML**, **Markdown**, or **Plain Text**.

    subgraph Intelligence ["Dual-Intelligence AI Layer"]

### 🧠 Dual-Intelligence Engine Design
Writara guarantees **100% operational uptime**:
1. **Cloud Intelligence (Gemini 2.0 Flash):** High-speed, multimodal-grade generative inference with rich nuance, deep vocabulary, and complex reasoning.
2. **Local Algorithmic Fallback Engine:** If an API key is missing, network access is severed, or quota limits are encountered, Writara transparently shifts to its local algorithmic engine. **The application never crashes, never throws blank screens, and never halts user workflows.**

## 🎯 Judge's 3-Minute Evaluation Script

If you are a hackathon judge or reviewer evaluating Writara, follow this quick 3-minute walkthrough:

1. **Launch the Platform:**
   Double-click `run-writara.bat` (or run `.\run-writara.ps1`). Open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. **Generate Master Content (15 seconds):**
   - Click **AI Generator** on the left sidebar.
   - Select **Blog Post & Article**, enter topic: `"The Future of AI in Clean Energy"`, and click **Generate Content Now**.
   - Notice the structured Markdown output, word count stats, and quality indicators.
3. **Execute 1-Click Repurposing (20 seconds):**
   - Click the pink **Repurpose (1-Click)** button right above your draft.
   - Watch Writara generate **LinkedIn, Twitter Thread, Instagram Carousel, Email Newsletter, Video Script, and Executive Summary** in real time.
4. **Run Quality & Anti-Hallucination Fact Audit (20 seconds):**
   - Click **Audit Quality**.
   - Review your Flesch Reading Ease score, Grade Level, and the **Anti-Hallucination Radar** flagging factual/statistical claims for citation.
5. **Inspect SEO & Google SERP Simulator (15 seconds):**
   - Click **SEO & SERP Suite** on the sidebar.
   - Toggle between **Desktop Preview** and **Mobile Preview** on Google.
   - Inspect the Schema.org JSON-LD structured snippet.
6. **Scan Clichés & Test Viral Headlines (20 seconds):**
   - Click **Headline A/B & Clichés**.
   - View 5 predicted CTR angles and inspect the AI Cliché Scanner detecting robotic phrases.
7. **Toggle Theme & Export (10 seconds):**
   - Click the **Sun/Moon icon** in the top navbar to toggle clean Academic Light Mode.
   - Open **Smart Editor** and click **Export -> Word (.docx)**.

## ⚡ Quickstart & Local Deployment

### Prerequisites
- **Node.js** (v18+ recommended, v24 verified)
- **Python** (v3.10+ recommended, v3.13 verified)
- **Git**

### Option 1: 1-Click Launch (Windows)
```powershell
# In the project root:
.\run-writara.ps1
```
*Or simply double-click `run-writara.bat`.*

### Option 2: Manual Terminal Setup

#### 1. Backend Server
```bash
cd backend
python -m venv venv

# Windows:
.\venv\Scripts\activate
# macOS/Linux:
source venv/bin/activate

pip install -r requirements.txt

# (Optional) Configure PostgreSQL in backend/.env:
# DATABASE_URL=postgresql://user:password@localhost:5432/writara

python -m uvicorn main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000 --reload
```

#### 2. Frontend Development Server
```bash
cd frontend
npm install
npm run dev
```

The application is now live at:
- **Frontend Studio:** [http://127.0.0.1:5173](http://127.0.0.1:5173)
- **FastAPI Backend:** [http://127.0.0.1:8000](http://127.0.0.1:8000)
- **Interactive Swagger Docs:** [http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs](http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs)

## 🔑 Optional: Configuring Your Gemini API Key

Writara operates straight out of the box using its built-in local algorithmic engine. To activate Gemini 2.0 Flash:
1. Click the **API Settings** pill in the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your Gemini API key (free at [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey)).
3. Click **Save Configuration**. Your key is securely saved in your browser's `localStorage` and never committed or transmitted to any third party.

## 📡 REST API Documentation

Writara provides a full OpenAPI 3.0 specification accessible at `/docs`. Key endpoints include:

```
POST   /api/generate              Generate long/short-form content with voice constraints
POST   /api/edit                  Perform in-line smart actions (rewrite, shorten, expand, grammar)
POST   /api/repurpose             Convert 1 master draft into 6 platform-native formats
POST   /api/evaluate              Compute Flesch scores, adherence metrics, and fact flags
POST   /api/seo/analyze           Generate SERP simulation, meta metrics, and Schema JSON-LD
POST   /api/headlines/ab-test     Produce 5 viral headline formulas with predicted CTR
POST   /api/cliches/scan          Detect AI tropes, calculate originality score
GET    /api/brand-voice           Retrieve all saved brand voice personas
POST   /api/brand-voice/analyze   Reverse-engineer brand voice DNA from raw sample text
GET    /api/documents             List saved drafts (filter by project, search, tags)
POST   /api/documents             Create and store content drafts
GET    /api/calendar/posts        Retrieve scheduled social posts
POST   /api/calendar/posts        Schedule content on the multi-channel calendar
GET    /api/analytics             Aggregate word velocity, time saved, and copywriter ROI
```
